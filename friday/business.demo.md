# Suryoday Solar

What we do: rooftop solar for homes, shops and small offices across India. We
survey the roof, install the panels, handle the government subsidy papers and
service the system for five years.

## What we sell

- 3 kW home plan — ₹1,85,000 before subsidy; about ₹1,07,000 after the PM Surya Ghar subsidy. Cuts a typical home bill by about 80%.
- 5 kW home plan — ₹2,90,000 before subsidy; about ₹2,12,000 after subsidy.
- 10 kW shop and office plan — ₹5,40,000; no home subsidy, but faster payback for daytime businesses.
- Free site survey — an engineer visits, checks the roof and gives a written quote.

## Offers

- No-cost EMI for 12 months on all home plans, through our bank partner.
- Free cleaning visits for the first year.

## Meetings

- Hours: Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM.
- Length: 30 minutes.
- Where: a site survey at the client's address, or a video call on Google Meet.

## Common questions

- How long does installation take? — Three to five days after the survey.
- Is there a warranty? — 25 years on the panels and 5 years on the full system.
- Does it work in the rainy season? — Yes, it produces less on cloudy days but still works; the grid covers the rest.
- How does the subsidy work? — We file the papers; the subsidy goes straight to the client's bank account.

## Never

- Never promise a discount, a delivery date or a refund that is not written here.
